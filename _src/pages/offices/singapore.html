Where is TechNext's Singapore office? {{icon:chevron}}

Our headquarters is at 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore 180261. Email sales@technext.asia or message us on WhatsApp at +65 8839 6998 to arrange a meeting.

What does the Singapore HQ do? {{icon:chevron}}

Sales, discovery workshops and on-site work with clients in Singapore. Configuration, custom modules and integration run from our main development and consulting hub in Taguig City, Metro Manila, and our AI engineers in Ho Chi Minh City join when AI is in scope.

Does TechNext work on-site in Singapore? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. We work on-site with clients in Singapore, and remotely with clients in 10+ countries.

How do I start an Odoo project with TechNext? {{icon:chevron}}

Book a discovery call, email sales@technext.asia or use the quotation form. Most Odoo projects start with a discovery session that maps your processes to Odoo apps.
