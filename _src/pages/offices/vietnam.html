common questions

About the Ho Chi Minh City hub.

Where is TechNext's office in Vietnam? {{icon:chevron}}

At 62 Nguyễn Thị Nhung, Phường Hiệp Bình, Ho Chi Minh City. It's our AI engineering hub.

What does the Ho Chi Minh City hub do? {{icon:chevron}}

AI engineering: AI agents and automation, knowledge and RAG systems, chatbots and assistants, and local LLM deployment, often connected to Odoo. Odoo configuration, custom modules and integration run from our Taguig City hub.

Do the AI engineers work on every project? {{icon:chevron}}

They join when AI is in scope, which is decided in discovery. They work on the same project as the Singapore and Taguig City teams.

Can TechNext's AI work with Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Most of our AI work is connected to Odoo. See Odoo + AI integrations.

Does TechNext implement Odoo for companies in Vietnam? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. See Odoo in Vietnam for how we set up Odoo with the Vietnamese localisation: Circular 99 accounts, the 01/GTGT return and SInvoice e-invoices.
