What we collect

When you use the inquiry or quotation forms on this site we collect the details you enter: name, company, work email, phone number, the topic or scope you select, and your message. We do not collect payment details on this website.

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and, where you ask for one, prepare a quotation.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.
  • To operate and secure the website.

We do not sell personal data. We do not add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to.

Who processes it

Form submissions are recorded as an inquiry in our customer relationship system (Odoo CRM, hosted by Odoo) so our team can follow up, and we reply from sales@technext.asia. This website is hosted on Vercel. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. Each provider processes only what is needed to deliver its service.

The map on our Company page comes from Google Maps. It loads only when you choose Show the map, or on its own if you have accepted cookies; until then no request is made to Google Maps. Official Odoo videos on our app pages come from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.

Retention

We keep inquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a business record, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you may ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent to its use. Write to sales@technext.asia and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site, and Google Ads to measure whether our advertising leads to enquiries. These tools set cookies (such as _ga and _gcl_au) only after you choose Accept on the cookie notice. Until then Google Consent Mode keeps them switched off and Google receives only anonymous, cookieless signals.

Accepting also sets first-party tn_ cookies that remember your choice and record your visit count, the pages you viewed and how you arrived (referrer and campaign tags), for up to a year. Choosing Decline keeps all of these off; the site works the same either way. To change your mind, clear this site's cookies in your browser and the notice will appear again.

Nexi, the AI companion on this page, answers inside your browser: what you type is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is gone when you leave the page. Nexi only remembers whether you muted its sounds, in your browser's local storage (not a cookie).
