questions

Quick answers.

What exactly is free?

Our work: designing your website and setting it up in Odoo. We don't charge for it. Odoo itself is not free. Your website runs on your own Odoo subscription, which you pay to Odoo. We do this because many businesses that start with a website on Odoo later ask us to set up more apps. If you never do, that's fine.

Who owns the website and the data?

You do. The Odoo subscription and database are in your name, and you can export your data or move to another partner at any time.

How fast can my website be live?

In as little as 3 days once we have your logo, text and photos. Bigger websites can take a little longer, and we confirm the date on our first call.

Can I choose how it looks?

Yes. Pick a style, send us websites you like, or share your brand guide. We design every page around it and adjust it with you until it feels like your business.

I need an online shop or a special feature.

Tell us in the form. Your website design is still free. Online shops, bookings or custom features are quoted separately, in writing.
