          Itinerary: flights, hotel nights, transfers and tours are supplier services with a cost in the supplier's currency, placed on each day of a five-day Tokyo and Kyoto trip.

          Price and margin: costs in SGD, USD, JPY and EUR convert at sample rates; a markup per category sets the selling price, and the waterfall runs from supplier cost through markup and price to the margin left.

          Invoices and payments: a deposit is paid online when the quotation is signed and the balance is invoiced 30 days before departure. Each supplier gets a purchase order with its own payment term, and bills paid later in a foreign currency post an exchange difference.

          {{icon:check}} Sample services, costs and exchange rates; taxes left out. In Odoo, a markup like this is a pricelist rule based on cost, services can raise purchase orders when the quotation is confirmed, and the deposit is a prepayment on the online quotation.
