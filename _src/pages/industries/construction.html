        Budget and plan: the budget of S$ 1,250,000 is spread over twelve months as the planned value (PV) S-curve; approved variation orders add to the contract and the budget.

        Earned value: each month, the percentage of work done times the budget gives the earned value (EV); actual cost (AC) comes from the purchases, subcontractor bills and timesheets on the project's analytic account. CPI is EV divided by AC, SPI is EV divided by PV, and the estimate at completion is the budget divided by CPI.

        Progress claims: each month the certified work done to date, less retention (5% or 10%, limited to 5% of the contract sum) and less previous claims, is invoiced with GST at 9%.

        Subcontractors and retention: subcontractor claims are certified and billed to the project, with retention held from them; the retention ledger shows what the client holds from us and what we hold from subcontractors, and when each is released.

        {{icon:check}} Sample project and figures. Odoo records the budget, costs, progress and invoices per project; earned value is a report we build on that data (for example an Odoo spreadsheet dashboard), and how retention is set up (a retention line and account, or payment terms) is agreed in discovery.
