In one paragraph

Odoo integration connects the systems you keep to your Odoo database: banks, payment gateways, online stores such as Shopify and WooCommerce, marketplaces, carriers and specialist tools. TechNext uses standard connectors or the Odoo API, so orders, stock levels and payouts flow across without anyone re-typing them between systems.

under the hood

Odoo integration you can watch: webhooks, scheduled jobs, retries and a dead-letter queue.

A TechNext Odoo integration is more than a connector. Webhooks push events in seconds, scheduled jobs sync in batches, every message carries an idempotency key so nothing is booked twice, and failures retry with exponential backoff before a person is alerted. Take a connector down and watch the retries, the backlog and the recovery.

Sync log

    Connected to Odoo: bank feeds (statement lines, on a schedule), a payment gateway (payment events by webhook), a marketplace connector (orders by webhook, stock levels on a schedule), a courier (labels out, tracking by webhook), InvoiceNow through a Peppol access point (e-invoices out, delivery status polled), a BI or data warehouse (scheduled extracts) and a legacy WMS (polled for picks and confirmations).

    Reliability: each message carries an idempotency key, so a re-delivered event is recognised and ignored instead of creating a second order or payment. A failed call is retried with exponential backoff, 1, 2, 4 then 8 seconds in this demo; after the last try it moves to a dead-letter queue and a person is alerted. When the connector recovers, the backlog drains and dead letters can be replayed safely.

    common questions

    Integration, answered.

    Will Odoo replace our online store? {{icon:chevron}}

    Only if you want it to. Otherwise we connect your existing Shopify, WooCommerce or marketplace store so orders and stock stay in step both ways.

    Do you build custom connectors? {{icon:chevron}}

    Only where no maintained connector exists. We prefer standard connectors from Odoo and its app store, and we document any custom code we write.

    One-way or two-way sync? {{icon:chevron}}

    Either. We agree the direction of every flow up front, field by field, so both systems hold the version of the truth you expect.

    What about payment fees and payouts? {{icon:chevron}}

    They are captured and reconciled, so your books match what the gateway actually paid out — fees included.

    What happens if a sync fails? {{icon:chevron}}

    Error handling alerts a person rather than failing silently, and everything is tested on staging with real records before it goes live.

    How is integration priced? {{icon:chevron}}

    On quotation, based on the systems and flows involved — request a quotation.
