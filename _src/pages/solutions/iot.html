In one paragraph

IoT (Internet of Things) solutions connect physical things such as fridges, machines, meters, vehicles and rooms to software, so they report their own status. TechNext recommends the sensors and gateways, sets up the connection, builds the dashboards and alerts, and can feed the readings into Odoo, where they support maintenance, quality and inventory work. Odoo IoT also connects scales, printers and measuring tools directly to Odoo apps.

watch the sensors

IoT monitoring for a cold store, from the sensor to Odoo.

Three rooms report every minute. When one drifts out of range, the person on duty gets an alert; if it stays out too long, Odoo opens a quality check on the stock inside, and a power fault opens a maintenance request. Pick a scenario, move the alert limit, and hover a chart to read any minute.

    questions

    What teams ask before they start.

    Five questions that come in like alerts. Open one to read the reply.

    What can we monitor? {{icon:chevron}}

    Temperature and humidity, energy use, machine runtime and stoppages, doors, room occupancy and vehicle location are the common ones. If a sensor can measure it, it can usually be brought onto a dashboard.

    Do we need Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

    No. Dashboards and alerts work on their own. If you run Odoo, the readings can feed it, and Odoo IoT connects devices such as scales and printers to the Odoo apps directly.

    Who supplies the hardware? {{icon:chevron}}

    We recommend the sensors and gateways for your site and can source them, or work with devices you already have where they can be connected.

    What if a device goes offline? {{icon:chevron}}

    A device that stops reporting raises its own alert, so a silent sensor is noticed rather than mistaken for a calm reading.

    What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

    Pricing is on quotation, scoped to the number of devices, sites and integrations. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
