In one paragraph

Network services cover everything that connects your people and devices: the internet line, router and firewall, switches and cabling, Wi-Fi and remote access. TechNext plans the network for your office, installs and labels it, keeps staff, guest and device traffic apart, documents how it is set up and supports it afterwards, so changes are made safely and there is someone to call when something stops working.

An office network that lets the right traffic through.

    questions

    What teams ask before they start.

    Can you work with our existing equipment? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. We review what you have, keep what is sound and replace only what is holding you back.

    Do you set up remote access? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. A VPN or other secure remote access lets people reach office systems from home or on the road, with access limited to what they need.

    Can guests use our Wi-Fi without reaching our files? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. Guest Wi-Fi runs on its own network, kept apart from staff computers, servers and printers.

    Do you support the network after set-up? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes, through a support plan: changes, troubleshooting and regular checks, with the documentation kept current.

    What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

    Pricing is on quotation, scoped to your office, the equipment needed and the support you want. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
