In one paragraph

TechNext designs the brand assets a company uses every day: a logo refresh and guidelines, sales decks, brochures, and social and document templates. Everything is built as editable PowerPoint, Canva, Figma or InDesign files your team can use without a designer, and handed over completely with files, fonts and rights.

one change, every asset

Graphic design and brand assets, built as one system.

TechNext designs your brand as tokens first: colours, a type pair, logo lock-ups, spacing and corner radius. Components are built from the tokens and every asset from the components, so one decision updates the business card, the social post, the email signature, the slide and the signage together. Switch between three sample directions for a fictional client and watch the change travel through, while each channel's export checklist ticks off.

Tokens: four colours (primary, accent, surface, ink), a display and body type pair, logo lock-ups (horizontal, stacked, mark only), a spacing scale and a corner radius.

Components: button, tag, card, header and a brand pattern, each built only from the tokens.

Assets: slide, roll-up signage, business card, social post and email signature, built from the components, then exported per channel with the right sizes and formats, after contrast and licence checks.

common questions

Good to know.

Do we get editable files, or just PDFs? {{icon:chevron}}

Editable master files. PDFs are for sharing; the source files are yours so your team can make new assets later.

Which tools are the files made in? {{icon:chevron}}

PowerPoint, Canva, Figma or InDesign — whichever your team already uses, so you can keep working without a designer.

Can you refresh our logo instead of replacing it? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. A refresh that keeps what works is often the right call, and we'll say so if it is.

Can our team make new assets afterwards? {{icon:chevron}}

That's the point of the templates and the guideline — new decks, posts and documents that stay on-brand without starting from scratch.

Do we own the fonts and files? {{icon:chevron}}

You own everything delivered. Any licensed fonts are noted at hand-over so you know what is included.

How is it priced? {{icon:chevron}}

Per scope, as a one-off. We quote before work begins, so you see the figure first. Get a quotation.
