    Latest events

      Sell: each store's Point of Sale sells the product and its stock goes down.

      Reorder: each store has a reordering rule (minimum and maximum). When the forecast (on hand plus incoming, less outgoing) drops below the minimum, Odoo raises a transfer from the central warehouse for the quantity that brings it back to the maximum.

      Transfer: the warehouse reserves the stock, picks and ships it through a transit location, and the store receives it. When the warehouse has only part of the quantity, it ships what it has and keeps a backorder for the rest.

      Buy: the warehouse has its own reordering rule with the Buy route. Falling below its minimum raises a request for quotation to the vendor; the buyer confirms it and the goods arrive after the vendor's lead time.

      Exceptions: a promotion weekend multiplies demand at chosen stores, shelves can run empty, and stock can be moved from a store with surplus while the warehouse waits for the vendor.

      Sample data, one product, time compressed. In Odoo the rules run automatically on a schedule you choose or from the replenishment report; Odoo 20 can suggest minimum and maximum levels from demand history, and this demo uses a simple sample rule for them.

      common questions

      Odoo for retailers, answered.

      Does Odoo Point of Sale work offline?

      Yes. Odoo Point of Sale keeps selling through internet outages and syncs orders when the connection returns. Card payments depend on your terminal's own connection.

      Can Odoo manage stock across several stores?

      Yes. Each store is its own warehouse or location with live stock, transfers between stores and reordering rules per store.

      Can we run loyalty and promotions in Odoo?

      Yes. Loyalty programs, gift cards, eWallets, coupons and promotions are set up once and apply at every till, and online if you use Odoo eCommerce.

      Do daily takings post to accounting automatically?

      Yes. Closing a POS session posts sales, taxes and payment methods to Accounting, and card settlements reconcile from the bank feed.

      Where does TechNext start with a retailer?

      With one store: products, POS, stock and accounting go live first, then the same setup rolls out store by store.
