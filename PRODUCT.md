# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners, finance leads and operations managers of growing companies (SMEs to mid-size groups) in Singapore, the Philippines and nearby markets who have outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools. They arrive from search, ads or referrals, usually comparing implementation partners, and want to know: can this team set up Odoo properly, and will they stay after go-live?

## Product Purpose
TechNext Pte. Ltd. implements Odoo ERP end to end (discovery, configuration, migration, training, integration, support), and also builds enterprise AI, websites and brand assets, and IoT / app / network services. The site's job is to earn a discovery call or a free-website-with-Odoo enquiry.

## Positioning
"You don't need to hire your own team": one team covers Odoo, the website and brand built on it, and the tech around it, from Singapore (HQ), Taguig City (main development and consulting hub) and Ho Chi Minh City (AI engineering hub), and stays on after go-live.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by `_src/build.py`; partials in `_src/pages/`; Vercel with strict CSP (inline scripts by hash only, no `on*=` handlers).
- Booking modal + chat live in `booking-modal.js`; forms post to `/api/lead` (Odoo CRM).
- Light theme only (no dark mode).
- Google tags must stay in the homepage head.

## Brand Commitments
- Name TechNext; brand blue #3167CA; existing logo files in `assets/img/`.
- Partner wording: "Odoo Ready Partner", never "Certified". Also a Claude Partner.
- Approved figures only: 10+ countries, 11+ enterprise clients, 4 AI disciplines. No ISO claims, no founding year, no project counts, no pricing.
- Odoo is never "free": only TechNext's design/setup labour is free; the client pays the Odoo subscription.
- Odoo app icons are shown flat (Odoo-style, no tiles).
- Current copy and claims stay as written (owner, 2026-10-17).

## Evidence on Hand
Real Odoo app icons, Odoo screenshots (odoocdn), office details (SG/PH/VN), 20 blog articles, 8 industry pages, 50 Odoo app pages, case-studies page. No client testimonials or logos on the home page; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Prove with Odoo itself: real screens, real hand-offs, not abstract claims.
2. One team, end to end: show the continuity from discovery to support.
3. Plain language for business owners, not ERP jargon.
4. Every page leads to one clear next step (discovery call or free website enquiry).
