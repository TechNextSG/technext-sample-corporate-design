Odoo CRM: leads, pipeline and activities. TechNext, an Odoo Ready Partner headquartered in Singapore with teams in the Philippines and Vietnam, sets it up on a staging copy of your database, migrates your data, trains the people who use it and supports it after go-live.

In Odoo 20, lead generation uses Dun & Bradstreet company data instead of Clearbit, a dropdown switches between teams' pipelines, and assignment rules keep each salesperson in rotation, in rotation with a limit, or out of it.     Attractive quotations in two clicks

    Create professional-looking quotations in no time with the user-friendly interface of our sales tool. Select your products from the catalog and let Odoo handle the rest.

    05 · CRM

    Turn reporting data into money

    Smart decisions for your sales strategy should be based on accurate and real-time data. Dig deeper into your sales metrics with revenue forecasts, team performance analysis, and custom dashboards.
